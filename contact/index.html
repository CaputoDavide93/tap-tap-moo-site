Common questions

How do I get to the settings?

Hold the small, faint button in the corner of the sky, then answer the sum on the keypad. It is hard for little fingers on purpose.

How do I keep my toddler inside the game?

On Android, turn on Pin the screen to this app under Little fingers in the settings. On iPhone and iPad, turn on Guided Access once in Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access, then triple-click the side button while playing and tap Start. The settings explain both.

Why don’t I hear anything?

Under one year the game starts silent, as health guidance suggests. Turn on Say each word in the settings. On iPhone, the silent switch mutes the game too.

How do I move to a new phone?

Choose your child’s age and stages again on the new phone; it takes a minute. Your recordings stay on the old phone, so record the words again if you use your own voice.

I said thanks on another phone.

Settings → Say thanks → Restore purchase, signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account.
