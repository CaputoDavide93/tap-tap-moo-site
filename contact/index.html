Common questions

How do I get to the settings?

Hold the small, faint lock in the top corner of the sky for two seconds. Links out, the microphone and the photo picker then ask for a simple sum, like 7 + 5, on the keypad. It is quick for a grown-up and hard for little fingers, on purpose.

How do I keep my toddler inside the game?

The game already ignores the Android back gesture, and on iPhone and iPad a swipe from the top or bottom edge needs a second swipe to leave. The home gesture still works, because only the device can switch that off. To lock the device to the game on Android, turn on Pin the screen to this app under Look and safety → Little fingers in the settings. On iPhone and iPad, turn on Guided Access once in Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access, then triple-click the side button while playing and tap Start. The settings explain both.

Why don’t I hear anything?

Under one year the game starts silent, as health guidance suggests. Turn on Say each word under Sound and voice in the settings. Check the phone’s volume, too. After each picture the voice waits a few seconds so you can say the word first; tap the picture to hear it straight away.

How do I move to a new phone?

Choose your child’s age and stages again on the new phone; it takes a minute. Your recordings and family photos stay on the old phone, so add them again if you use them.

Is there anything to buy?

No. Tap Tap Moo has no purchases, no subscription and no ads. Your own voice, family photos, a second language and every app icon are there for everyone.
