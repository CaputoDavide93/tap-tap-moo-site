Tap the sky, and say it together.

Tap Tap Moo is a calm first-words moment for you and your little one, in the day’s routines: before story time, before a meal, at potty time. On iPhone, iPad and Android.

  • You hold the phone, your little one taps, and one picture blooms on a twinkling sky. The app waits, so you say the word first; then a calm voice says it too.
  • Made for the whole family: brothers and sisters join in, and supporters add their own voice and family photos.
  • Grows with your child: weaning, first words, bath and bed, potty training, getting dressed and more.
  • English and Italian, each with its own offline voice.
  • Nothing leaves your device. No internet, no ads, no analytics, no account.
  • Free and complete, with an optional one-off “Say thanks” purchase that lets you use your own voice and photos of your own family and pets.
