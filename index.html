Tap the sky, and something magical appears.

Tap Tap Moo is a calm first-words game for babies and toddlers on iPhone, iPad and Android, made for playing together.

  • One thing at a time on a pale, twinkling sky, and a calm voice that says its name. A cow says “Moo!”, then “a brown cow”, then “cow”.
  • Grows with your child: weaning, first words, bath and bed, potty training, getting dressed and more.
  • English and Italian, each with its own offline voice.
  • Nothing leaves your device. No internet, no ads, no analytics, no account.
  • Free and complete, with an optional one-off “Say thanks” purchase.
