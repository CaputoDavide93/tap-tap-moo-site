Why Tap Tap Moo exists

Why it exists

You have a baby on your lap and a few minutes to fill. Most apps for small children flash, beep and hand out rewards, and a toddler’s first tap lands on a button that opens something else.

Tap Tap Moo shows one thing on a quiet sky and says its name, so you can say it together. It leaves out ads, scores, streaks and anything that asks your child for more.

Why use it

One thing at a time

A few taps bring one picture and its word. Nothing races and nothing keeps score.

Made for the two of you

You sit with your child and say the words. Play time ends on a calm goodbye screen.

It stays on your phone

No account, internet connection or ads. Your settings, photos and recordings stay on this device.

You decide

Stages, sound, pace and play time sit behind the grown-ups’ gate, and you set them.

A few calm minutes, together.
