Privacy policy

Tap Tap Moo doesn’t collect any data about you or your child. Everything the app keeps stays on your phone or tablet.

Effective 5 October 2026. Applies to the Tap Tap Moo apps for iPhone, iPad and Android, and to this website.

In short

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No servers. The app has no server and no code of its own that sends anything over the internet.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking. No third-party SDKs for any of these.
  • No AI in the app. What appears next comes from simple rules that run on your device.
  • Nothing leaves the device unless you choose to email us.

What the app keeps, and where

The app keeps these in its own private storage on your device. The developer never receives them and cannot see them.

  • Settings for each child: an age band (a choice of defaults, not a birth date), and the colours, sound, stages, themes, pace, play time, language, bedtime and appearance you chose.
  • Your child’s first name or nickname, if you give one: kept in its own file, apart from the settings, shown only in the grown-ups’ settings, and never sent, logged or backed up.
  • Today’s words: which words the voice said today for each child, kept for one day, so you can see what you practised.
  • Your own recordings, if you choose to make them: one short audio file per word.
  • Your family photos, if you choose them: each one squared, made smaller, and saved without its location or camera details, with the word it stands for and any name you give it.

On Android the app turns off Google backup and device-to-device transfer for all of this. On iPhone and iPad it leaves your recordings, your photos and the names you give (your child’s, and your photos’) out of iCloud and computer backups; your settings can be part of your own iCloud or computer backup, which only you can open. The trade-off: on a new Android phone, or after a reset, you set the game up again, and add your recordings and photos again.

The voice

The voice in every language is Allison, from ElevenLabs. Every word is made ahead of time with ElevenLabs’ text-to-speech, before each version of the app is released, and comes inside the app. The app never contacts ElevenLabs, and nothing you or your child do reaches them.

Permissions, all optional

The game works fully without any permission.

  • Microphone: asked for only the first time you tap a record button in the grown-ups’ settings and answer the sum, never before and never on the play screen. The app records only while you record a word, for at most five seconds, and keeps the file on your device. It doesn’t analyse, upload or share your recordings. You can turn the permission off in your phone’s settings, and the game goes back to its own voice.

The app never asks for your photo library, location, contacts, camera, calendar, notifications or motion data. When you add a family photo, after the sum, the phone’s own photo picker opens; you choose one photo, and only that photo reaches the app.

Things you share into the app

Only what you choose to add: recordings of your voice, and photos of your family and pets picked with the phone’s own picker. The app accepts nothing shared from other apps. It doesn’t analyse, upload or share what you add.

Age checks by the app stores

Where a law requires it (Texas, for example), the app asks the App Store or Google Play for a signal about the account’s age. It uses the answer only to decide, there and then, whether the game may be shown, and never stores or sends it.

Links

The grown-ups’ settings link to this privacy policy, the contact page and the sources of the milestones for grown-ups. Each opens only when you tap it and answer the sum, in your browser. The app never fetches them itself.

Payments

None. Tap Tap Moo has no purchases, no subscription and no ads, and nothing in it is locked. The app contains no store billing or payment library, so nothing about payments is collected or sent.

Deleting your data

Settings → About → Reset all settings removes every child’s settings and name, today’s words, your recordings and your photos. Settings → Sound and voice → Record words → Delete all recordings removes just the recordings, and Settings → Family and extras → Choose photos → Delete all photos just the photos. Deleting the app removes everything.

Children

Tap Tap Moo is made for children under five, played with a grown-up. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children, and shows no ads. The play screen has no links, purchases or prompts; the only way out is the grown-ups’ button, which has to be held for two seconds; links, the microphone and the photo picker then ask for a sum.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics, scripts or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under its privacy statement.

If you contact us

If you email for support, your email address and message are used only to reply to you, and aren’t shared with anyone. Please leave out your child’s name.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. A change that means data leaves your device in a new way would also be explained in the app before it takes effect.
