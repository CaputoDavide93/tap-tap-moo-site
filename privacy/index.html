Privacy policy

Tap Tap Moo is a game for babies and toddlers. It collects nothing, sends nothing and has no account. This page says exactly what it keeps on your device, and why.

Last updated 27 September 2026

In short

  • No personal data is collected, shared or sold, by the developer or anyone else.
  • The app does not connect to the internet. There is no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking.
  • What the app saves stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall it.
  • The only permission you are ever asked for is the microphone, and only if a supporter chooses to record the words in their own voice.

What the app keeps on your device

WhatWhyHow to remove it
Your settings for each child: an optional first name, the age band (a choice of defaults, not a birth date), colours, sound, stages, themes, play time, language, bedtime and appearanceSo the game opens the way you left itSettings → Reset all settings, or uninstall

That is the whole list, and none of it is sent anywhere by the app. On Android the app’s data is left out of Google and device-to-device backups. On iPhone and iPad your recordings are left out of backups; your settings may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup, which only you can open.

The microphone

Tap Tap Moo asks for the microphone only the first time a supporter taps a record button in the grown-ups’ settings, never before and never on the play screen. It records only while you are recording a word, for at most five seconds, and saves the recording as a small audio file in the app’s own folder. Recordings are not analysed, uploaded or shared. You can turn the permission off at any time in your phone’s settings; the game then uses its own voice.

Purchases

The optional “Say thanks” purchase goes through the App Store or Google Play, which handle the payment under their own privacy policies. The developer receives no personal information about who bought it. The purchase is offered only in the grown-ups’ settings, behind a held button and a sum, and never to the child.

Children

The app is made for children under five, played with a grown-up. It collects no personal information from children or anyone else, shows no advertising, and has no links, purchases or prompts on the play screen. Everything a child could reach sits behind the grown-ups’ gate. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to request, correct or delete from the developer; everything the app keeps is on your device and under your control, as listed above.

Links out of the app

The grown-ups’ settings link to this page and to the contact page. They open in your web browser, which is outside the app. This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or scripts; GitHub, which hosts it, may keep standard server logs.

What the phone itself records

Your phone may note that the app was opened (for example in Screen Time or Digital Wellbeing). The app has no say in that.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date. Questions are welcome at caputodav@gmail.com or on the contact page.
