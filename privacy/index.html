Privacy policy

Tap Tap Moo doesn’t collect any data about you or your child. Everything the app keeps stays on your phone or tablet.

Effective 27 September 2026. Applies to the Tap Tap Moo apps for iPhone, iPad and Android, and to this website.

In short

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No servers. The app has no server and no code of its own that sends anything over the internet.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking. No third-party SDKs for any of these.
  • No AI. What appears next comes from simple rules that run on your device.
  • Nothing leaves the device unless you choose to email us.

What the app keeps, and where

The app keeps these in its own private storage on your device. The developer never receives them and cannot see them.

  • Settings for each child: an optional first name, an age band (a choice of defaults, not a birth date), and the colours, sound, stages, themes, pace, play time, language, bedtime and appearance you chose.
  • Today’s words: which words the voice said today for each child, kept for one day, so you can see what you practised.
  • Your own recordings, if you are a supporter and choose to make them: one short audio file per word.
  • Whether you said thanks: one yes or no, so the thank-you stays unlocked.

On Android the app turns off Google backup and device-to-device transfer for all of this. On iPhone and iPad it leaves your recordings out of iCloud and computer backups; your settings can be part of your own iCloud or computer backup, which only you can open. The trade-off: on a new Android phone, or after a reset, you set the game up again and record your words again.

Permissions, all optional

The game works fully without any permission.

  • Microphone: asked for only the first time a supporter taps a record button in the grown-ups’ settings, never before and never on the play screen. The app records only while you record a word, for at most five seconds, and keeps the file on your device. It doesn’t analyse, upload or share your recordings. You can turn the permission off in your phone’s settings, and the game goes back to its own voice.

The app never asks for your location, contacts, camera, photo library, calendar, notifications or motion data.

Things you share into the app

None. The app accepts nothing from other apps, and the only thing you add is your own voice, as above.

Links

The grown-ups’ settings have two links, to this privacy policy and to the contact page. They open only when you tap them, in your browser. The app never fetches them itself.

Saying thanks

The optional “Say thanks” purchase is paid through the App Store or Google Play under your store account. The developer never sees your name, card or account; the app stores only that the purchase exists. It is offered only in the grown-ups’ settings, behind a held button and a sum, never to your child.

On Android, the purchase uses Google Play’s billing library, which talks to the Play Store app on your phone and may send its own diagnostics (such as billing errors) to Google. The developer doesn’t receive or control them, and they contain nothing about your child or your words. Google’s privacy policy applies. On iPhone and iPad, Apple handles the purchase under its privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Settings → Reset all settings removes every child’s settings, today’s words and your recordings. Settings → Your own voice → Delete all recordings removes just the recordings. Deleting the app removes everything. The thank-you stays with your store account; “Restore purchase” brings it back.

Children

Tap Tap Moo is made for children under five, played with a grown-up. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children, and shows no ads. The play screen has no links, purchases or prompts; the only way out is the grown-ups’ button, which has to be held, and then a sum.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics, scripts or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under its privacy statement.

If you contact us

If you email for support, your email address and message are used only to reply to you, and aren’t shared with anyone. Please leave out your child’s name.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. A change that means data leaves your device in a new way would also be explained in the app before it takes effect.
